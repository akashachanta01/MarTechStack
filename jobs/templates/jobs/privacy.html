Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

MarTechJobs.io ("we", "us") is a job board for marketing technology professionals. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it. We keep this deliberately short and plain.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you subscribe to job alerts. We use it solely to send you relevant MarTech job updates.
  • Basic analytics — anonymized usage data (pages visited, approximate location) to improve the site. We do not sell this.

How we use your data

  • To send the job alerts you signed up for.
  • To understand which roles and pages are useful so we can improve the board.

We never sell your email address or personal data to third parties.

Job listings

Job postings on MarTechJobs are aggregated from publicly available company career pages and applicant tracking systems. We link directly to the employer's official application page.

Unsubscribing

Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can remove yourself anytime at our unsubscribe page. When you unsubscribe, your email is deleted from our list.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us via the contact page and we'll respond promptly.

This is a standard privacy policy for a job board. For specific legal compliance (GDPR, CCPA), consult a professional.
