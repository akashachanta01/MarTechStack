Why we exist

A job board that actually speaks MarTech

Built by a practitioner, for the people who run the marketing stack — not the ones who just list it on a résumé.

The problem

Generic job boards don't understand what we do

A while back I was looking for a Marketing Operations role. I went to the big boards, searched "Marketing Automation," and got buried — social media managers who "post to Facebook," sales associates who "send emails," hundreds of listings to sift through just to find one genuine technical role.

They don't know the difference between a HubSpot admin and a content marketer. They treat "Marketo" as a keyword to stuff in a description — not a hard skill it takes years to master.

What you get here

Our purpose

Give MarTech specialists a place that gets them

MarTechJobs exists to be the high-signal home for marketing technology careers — a focused board where every role is relevant, organized by stack, and worth your time. No recruiter fluff. No generic noise. Just the technical work you came for.

It isn't a corporation with a sales team. It's a focused project built to solve one specific problem really well — and to stay honest while doing it.

What we stand for

Four rules we don't break

Zero noise

Every job is screened — AI plus review. If it isn't a real MarTech role, it doesn't make the cut.

Organized by stack

We categorize by tool, not just title — because knowing Salesforce is a different job than knowing HubSpot.

Transparency

We surface salary wherever a listing provides it — and we never fabricate a number. You shouldn't have to guess.

Always free for job seekers

Browsing is free, forever. We're vendor-neutral — no pay-to-play rankings, ever.
