The Origin Story

I built this because I was tired of the noise.

Hi, I'm the founder of MarTechJobs.

A while back, I was looking for a new role in Marketing Operations. I went to the big job boards — LinkedIn, Indeed, the usual suspects — and typed in "Marketing Automation".

The results were a mess.

I saw "Social Media Manager" roles that required "posting to Facebook." I saw "Sales Associates" who just needed to "send emails." I had to sift through hundreds of irrelevant listings just to find one genuine technical role.

I realized that generic job boards don't understand what we do.

They don't know the difference between a HubSpot Admin and a Content Marketer. They don't know that "Marketo" is a hard skill, not just a keyword to stuff in a description.

So I built the solution.

MarTechJobs isn't a big corporation with a sales team. It's a passion project built to solve that specific problem. It follows three simple rules:

1
Zero Noise. Every single job is screened using AI and manual review. If it's not a real MarTech role, it gets deleted. No "Social Media Ninjas" allowed.
2
Respect for your craft. We categorize jobs by Tech Stack, not just title. Because knowing Salesforce is different from knowing HubSpot.
3
Transparency first. We prioritize listings that show salary data. You shouldn't have to guess what a role is worth.

If you're here, you're probably one of us — someone who sits at the intersection of data, strategy, and technology.

Welcome home.

— The Founder
