Common Questions

Everything you need to know about MarTech careers

What is a MarTech job?
A MarTech (marketing technology) job involves working with software platforms that help companies plan, execute, and measure marketing campaigns. Common roles include Marketing Operations Manager, Marketing Technologist, Salesforce Administrator, HubSpot Specialist, and CDP Analyst. Most roles sit at the intersection of marketing strategy and technical implementation.
How much do marketing technology jobs pay?
Marketing technology salaries vary by role and seniority. Marketing Operations Managers earn $85K–$130K, Salesforce Architects earn $140K–$200K, Marketing Technologists earn $90K–$145K, and Revenue Operations Directors earn $160K–$230K. See our full MarTech Salary Guide covering 40+ roles.
Are there remote marketing technology jobs?
Yes — over 180 of the 700+ roles on MarTechJobs.io are fully remote. Popular remote searches include Remote Salesforce Jobs, Remote Adobe Experience Cloud Jobs, and Remote Braze Jobs. Use the remote filter on any search to narrow results.
How do I break into marketing technology?
Get certified on a major platform (Salesforce, HubSpot, or Adobe are the best starting points), build hands-on experience with campaign operations or CRM administration, and set a free MarTech job alert for your target role and location. Most hiring managers value platform certifications and demonstrable project experience over traditional degrees.
