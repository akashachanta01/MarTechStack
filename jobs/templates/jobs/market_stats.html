How is this data collected?

Listings are pulled daily from company hiring systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters and Workable), then screened so only genuine Marketing Operations, marketing automation, MarTech engineering and marketing analytics roles are counted. Closed and filled roles are removed daily. The report is saved each month, so next month's edition shows what changed.
