    Why a MarTech-specific ATS checker?

    Platform names are the filter

    Marketing Ops job descriptions list specific platforms — Marketo Engage, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, HubSpot, Segment — and ATS filters look for those literal names. Generic resume tools treat them as ordinary words; this checker knows the MarTech stack.

    Rebrands break keyword matches

    Pardot is now Marketing Cloud Account Engagement. Marketo is Adobe Marketo Engage. If the job uses the new name and your resume uses the old one, a literal match can miss it. The wording-fix section catches this.

    Which gaps matter most

    Missing skills are ranked by how many live MarTech jobs on this site ask for them, so you fix the gaps that help across the market first, not just for one posting.
