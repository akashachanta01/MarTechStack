Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MarTechJobs.io.

Is it free for job seekers?
−
Yes, completely free. Browsing jobs, setting up alerts, and applying to roles costs nothing for job seekers. We make money from employers who post job listings — never from candidates.
How do I post a job?
+
Click “Post a Job” in the top navigation. Standard listings start at $99 and are live within 24 hours. Featured listings (with highlighted placement) are available from $199. We accept card payments via Stripe.
How often are jobs updated?
+
New jobs are added every day, seven days a week. We also run a daily link verification process — any posting with a broken or expired application link is automatically flagged and removed. You’ll never click a dead link.
What MarTech tools are covered?
+
We cover the full MarTech landscape — marketing automation (HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot, Braze, Klaviyo), CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot CRM), analytics (Google Analytics, Amplitude, Adobe Analytics, Mixpanel), CDP (Segment, Rudderstack), and more.
How do job alerts work?
+
After signing up (free), configure custom alerts by keyword, tool, location, job type, or salary band. Choose daily or weekly digest emails. You’ll only receive alerts for roles that match your specific criteria.
Can I post remote-only roles?
+
Absolutely. Remote roles are fully supported and get a dedicated “Remote” badge in listings. Specify remote-only, hybrid, or fully on-site. We also have a dedicated Remote Jobs section that showcases location-flexible roles.
