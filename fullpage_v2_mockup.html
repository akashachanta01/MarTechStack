Frequently Asked Questions

Is MarTechJobs free to use for job seekers? −

Yes, completely free. Browse, search, and apply to all jobs at no cost. Create a free account to set up job alerts and save listings.

How do I post a job? −

Click 'Post a Job' in the navigation. Choose your package, fill in the job details, and your listing goes live within minutes after review.

How often are jobs updated? +

New jobs are added daily. We verify all links and remove expired listings every 24 hours to ensure freshness.

What marketing tools does this cover? +

We cover the full MarTech stack: HubSpot, Salesforce, Marketo, Segment, Amplitude, Klaviyo, Braze, and 50+ other platforms.

How do job alerts work? +

Subscribe with your email and set preferences (role type, location, tools). We send a curated digest of matching new jobs — daily or weekly, your choice.

Can I post a remote job? +

Absolutely. Remote roles get highlighted with a 'Remote' badge and appear in our dedicated remote jobs section, giving them extra visibility.
